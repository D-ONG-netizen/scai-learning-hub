Mathematical Modeling 1 Courseware Sample

Week 6: Derivative rules and the chain rule

Key ideas:
- The derivative measures an instantaneous rate of change.
- Product, quotient, and chain rules are used to differentiate composite expressions.
- A common error is forgetting the inner derivative in a composite function.

Review questions:
1. Differentiate sin(x^2).
2. Differentiate e^(3x) ln(x).
3. Explain why the chain rule depends on the composition of functions.

Mind map:
Derivative rules -> constant, power, product, quotient, chain
Applications -> velocity, related rates, approximation
Common errors -> missing inner derivative, incorrect signs, mixing variables
